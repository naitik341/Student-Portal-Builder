Add a "Reviews" section to the MessMate manager dashboard: an Overall Review at the top, an AI bot (MessBot) directly under it, and a full list of every review below. Reuse the existing theme (cream #F1ECEC background, orange #F06A14, black 3px outlines, Archivo Black headings, Modak for the big title). Every button uses the same clicky press behavior as the rest of the app (scale 0.93, spring back, vibrate).

ROUTE: /manager/reviews (add a "Reviews" button in the dashboard header next to "Edit Menu")

LAYOUT (single scrollable page, top to bottom)

1) HEADER
- Back circle button, title "REVIEWS" (Archivo Black, black, with "REVIEWS" in Modak orange + black outline like the ORDER? heading).
- Filter row of pills: Period (Today | 7 Days | 30 Days | All), Meal (All | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner), Item (dropdown from menu items). Selected pill = black with white text, others = white with black outline. Animated sliding highlight. All sections below update when filters change.

2) OVERALL REVIEW CARD (white card, 3px black border, 18px radius)
- Big average score out of 5 with count-up animation, inside a ring colored by score (red < 3, amber 3-4, green > 4).
- The 5 rating faces (same style as student side, small, read-only) with horizontal bars showing the count and percentage for each rating 1-5. Bars grow from 0 on load.
- Total reviews count and trend vs previous period ("+0.3 vs last week", green up or red down arrow).
- Mini row of 3 meal scores (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) as small cards. Tapping one applies the Meal filter.
- "Best rated item" and "Needs attention item" chips computed from the data (item with highest and lowest average).
- Top 5 tags as chips with counts (red chips for complaints, green for praise). Tapping a tag filters the review list below to that tag.

3) MESSBOT CARD (directly under the overall card, this is the hero)
- Orange card with black outline and a cute robot avatar (Lucide Bot icon in a black circle) on the left. Title "MessBot says" in Archivo Black.
- Auto-generates when the page loads and whenever filters change (debounced, cached per filter combo so it doesn't call the API repeatedly). While loading: shimmer skeleton plus rotating text ("Reading 143 reviews...", "Spotting patterns...", "Writing your summary...").
- Output appears with a typewriter effect, in this structure, under 100 words total:
  - VERDICT: one plain sentence with a mood emoji (e.g. "Overall a decent week, but lunch dal is dragging the score down. 😕")
  - 🔴 FIX THESE: top 3 issues, each with count, the item name, and one clear action ("Dal too salty (41). Cut salt in lunch dal.")
  - 🟢 KEEP DOING: 1-2 things students loved
  - 📈 TREND: one line comparing to the previous period
- Buttons under the output: "Regenerate", "Copy", "Share on WhatsApp" (wa.me link with the text prefilled).
- "ASK MESSBOT" mini-chat below the summary: 4 quick-question chips ("Why is lunch rated low?", "What do students like most?", "Any hygiene complaints?", "What should I fix first?") plus a text input. Tapping a chip or sending a question sends it to the AI with the currently filtered reviews as context. Answers appear as chat bubbles with a typing-dots animation. Keep answers under 60 words. Chat is session-only, not saved.
- If ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is missing or the call fails, fall back to a rule-based summary built from the stats so the card never shows an error. Show a small "Basic summary (AI offline)" label in that case.

4) ALL REVIEWS LIST
- Heading "ALL REVIEWS (143)" with a search box (searches comment text, tags and item names) and a sort dropdown: Newest | Oldest | Lowest rating | Highest rating.
- Quick filter pills: All | With comments | Complaints only | Praise only.
- Each review is a white card with black outline: the rating face (small static emoji-style face matching the rating), meal pill (yellow, orange or indigo), item names, tag chips, the comment text (expand "Read more" if longer than 3 lines), and time ago. Reviews are anonymous, never show a device ID.
- Cards stagger in. Load 20 at a time with a "LOAD MORE" orange pill button (or infinite scroll).
- Tap a card to open a detail sheet showing the full review with date/time.
- Manager actions in the sheet: "Mark as resolved" (moves the card to a subtle greyed style with a check) and "Flag as spam" (hides it from stats). Both persist.
- Friendly empty state with an illustration when no reviews match the filters ("No reviews here yet").
- Skeleton loaders while fetching.

BACKEND
- GET /api/reviews?period=&meal=&item=&tag=&q=&sort=&filter=&page=&limit= returns paginated reviews plus total count.
- GET /api/reviews/overview?period=&meal=&item= returns average, rating distribution, per-meal averages, per-item averages, top tags, trend vs previous period, best and worst item.
- POST /api/reviews/bot-summary with the same filters. Cached per filter combo for 10 minutes.
- POST /api/reviews/ask with { question, filters }.
- PATCH /api/reviews/:id with { resolved?, spam? }. Add resolved and spam boolean columns to the feedback table. Exclude spam from all stats.
